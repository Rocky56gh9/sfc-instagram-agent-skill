# SFC Central NJ: Brand Rules

Source of truth for every /ig-* skill in this fork. Installed at
`~/.claude/instagram/brand-rules.md` (symlinked to this file by `install.sh`).
If anything in a SKILL.md conflicts with this file, this file wins.

## Account

- Business: Sticky Fingers Cooking Central New Jersey (franchise of Sticky Fingers Cooking®)
- Owner: Chris Johnson
- Territory: ~30 miles around Princeton. Mercer, Middlesex, Somerset counties.
- Contact for CTAs: newjersey@stickyfingerscooking.com, (609) 301-2303 (voice/text)
- Brand marks: Sticky Fingers Cooking® and Silver Spatulas™ belong to the franchisor. Always use the ® / ™ on first mention in captions and on-screen cards.

## Audiences, in priority order

1. **Local parents.** Goal: they ask their school, PTO, library or rec center to bring SFC in (referral pull), or they book a camp or birthday party.
2. **Venue decision-makers** (PTO leads, principals, librarians, rec directors) who check the account before replying to outreach. Content must read as credible proof, not hype.
3. **Silver Spatulas** (activity directors, adult children of residents). Secondary. Do not mix into kids content in the same post.

## Program facts (use exactly; never contradict)

- Classes are 60 minutes.
- Every class centers on a weekly "Secret Ingredient" that drives that week's recipe. This is the signature device. Use it.
- Every class includes enrichment: STEM, cultural exploration, literacy, nutrition.
- All food allergies are accommodated. Recipes are always nut-free.
- No kitchen required: tables, chairs, an outlet, and access to a sink.
- Chef Instructors are background-checked and insured.
- Formats: after-school enrichment series, cooking camps (including day-off camps for school closures), private birthday parties (1.5 hours), Silver Spatulas for older adults.
- Kids ages 3-18.

## Proof points (approved)

- Teaching kids since 2011.
- Classes in schools, libraries, rec centers and community spaces across 10 states.
- Nationwide count: **pick one and use it consistently.** HQ graphic says "1 Million Kids Taught"; Silver Spatulas flyer says "over 1 million participants"; older outreach templates say "nearly 1 million." Until Chris confirms, use "nearly a million kids."
- Anything local (enrollment numbers, venues, re-enrollment rates) must come from Chris. Never invent it. Use `{{your number}}` and flag.

## Hard bans

- Never use: "facility cost", "cost-recovery", "revenue-share" (or "revenue share").
- No pricing in any public content. No dollar figures for classes, camps or parties.
- No PTO giveback, donation or commission terms in public content.
- No competitor names and no comparisons to other local programs.
- No venue named as a client unless Chris confirms the venue approved being named.
- No em dashes. (The humanizer strips them; do not write them in the first place.)

## Kids on camera

- **Do not script a shot that shows an identifiable child's face unless Chris confirms a photo/video release covers that class AND the venue allows filming.** Default shots: hands, food, pans, ingredients, aprons, chef hats from behind, the Chef Instructor.
- Never put a child's name, school name + class day, or schedule details together in the same post (location pattern risk).
- No filming at venues that prohibit it, regardless of release.

## Voice register (brand account)

- Warm, playful, food-pun forward. Puns are a house style, not decoration: "It's THYME", "Lettuce", "Donut worry", "TACO-bout", "'COOL'inary". One pun per caption or reel, max two. Never a pun in the hook if it makes the hook unclear.
- "Secret Ingredient" framing beats generic "cooking class" framing every time.
- Concrete over abstract: name the dish, the ingredient, the country.
- Parent-facing copy sells joy, confidence, and the kid trying something new. Venue-facing proof sells safety, logistics, enrichment.
- Hashtags: local first (#PrincetonNJ #MercerCounty #CentralJersey #SomersetCounty #MiddlesexCounty), then #SecretIngredient #KidsWhoCook. Three per post per /ig-caption's rule.

- Camps / day-off camps: link in bio.
- Birthday parties: DM or email newjersey@stickyfingerscooking.com.
